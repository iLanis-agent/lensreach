Lens Reach

A 50 mm lens on a sensor 23.5 mm wide frames like 76.6 mm on a 36 mm wide full frame sensor, a crop factor of 1.53 by width.

Boundary: arithmetic only Lens Reach works out equivalent focal length, coverage and distance from the numbers you type. The sensor size and the focal length are yours to read off your own camera and lens. It uses the simple distant-subject geometry, so it has not seen your lens and does not know its focusing behaviour, distortion, crop modes or stabilisation, and it gives no verdict that a lens or camera is right for a shot or for a purchase. Every number is your own entry.

Starting examples: a 50 mm lens on a 23.5 mm wide sensor, the scene it covers at 10 m, and the distance that fits a 3 m subject.
