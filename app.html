Lens Reach

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out equivalent focal length, coverage and distance from the numbers you type. The sensor size and the focal length are yours to read off your own camera and lens. It uses the simple distant-subject geometry, so it has not seen your lens and does not know its focusing behaviour, distortion, crop modes or stabilisation, and it gives no verdict that a lens or camera is right for a shot or for a purchase. Every number is your own entry.

The equivalent truth

What a lens frames like on a 36 mm wide full frame sensor. Sensor size and focal length are in mm.

The coverage truth

The width and height of the scene the lens covers at a distance, using the sensor and lens above.

The distance truth

How far away to stand to fit a subject of a given width across the sensor, using the sensor width and lens above.

Lens Reach is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
